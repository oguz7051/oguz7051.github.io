Common questions

How does Starlit work?
Pick a constellation (a project), choose a session length and tap Start focusing. When the session ends, a new star joins that constellation in your sky. If you stop early, the star falls.

Can I lock my phone during a session?
Yes. Starlit keeps time in the background, shows a live countdown on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and notifies you when time is up. Make sure notifications are allowed in Settings › Notifications › Starlit.

I don't see the Lock Screen timer.
Open Settings › Starlit and turn on Live Activities.

How do I change the language?
In the app: Me › Settings › Language.

I changed phones. How do I keep my sky?
Starlit has no account; your data stays on your device. On the old phone use Me › Settings › Export data to save a backup.

Do I need a subscription?
No. Starlit is a one-time purchase with every feature included. No ads, no in-app purchases.
