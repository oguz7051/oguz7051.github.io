Privacy Policy

Starlit: Focus Timer · Last updated October 31, 2026

Starlit does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Everything you create in Starlit (your constellations, focus sessions, notes, goals and settings) is stored only on your device. We never receive it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Notifications and Live Activities

Starlit uses local notifications and Live Activities to tell you when a session or break ends. They are created on your device; no data is sent to a server.

Exports and sharing

If you choose to export a backup or share an image of your sky, the file goes only where you send it through the system share sheet.

Third parties

Starlit contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

Children

Starlit does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
